Privacy Policy – Drape.Studio

Last updated: 2026-01-15

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Drape.Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our website and application.

Drape.Studio is operated from India and is primarily intended for users located in India. Users from other regions may access the service; such use is subject to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

We use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. When you sign in using Google, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture
  • Google account identifier (used internally for authentication)

This information is used solely to create and manage your account and provide access to the service.

We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google services.

b) Payment Information

Payments are processed by third-party payment gateways. We do not store your card details, bank account information, or payment credentials.

We may receive limited payment-related information such as:

  • Payment status
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Payment method type

This information is used only for billing, invoicing, and accounting purposes.

c) Usage and Analytics Data

We collect usage and interaction data to operate, maintain, and improve the service.

This may include:

  • Pages visited and actions taken within the application
  • Feature usage, button clicks, and navigation events
  • Approximate location (such as country or city level)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Referral or source information (for example, whether you arrived via a shared or invite-based link)

We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4) for this purpose. Analytics data may be associated with an internal user identifier after you sign in. We do not use analytics data for advertising, remarketing, or cross-site tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide access to the application and its features
  • Process payments and generate invoices
  • Communicate service-related updates
  • Understand usage patterns and improve product functionality
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Invite Links

Drape.Studio may use invite-based links to control access and measure interest in the service.

When you access the website using an invite link, we may record:

  • That the invite link was accessed
  • Whether it resulted in a successful sign-in or account creation

Invite-related data is used solely for internal analytics, access management, and service improvement.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share limited data with trusted third-party service providers only as necessary to operate the service, including:

  • Authentication providers (such as Google)
  • Payment processors
  • Cloud infrastructure and analytics providers

These providers process data on our behalf and are required to handle it securely and in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide and operate the service
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Drape.Studio uses cookies or similar technologies necessary for:

  • Authentication and session management
  • Security
  • Basic usage analytics

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the service

Requests can be made by contacting us at the email address below.

10. International Users

While Drape.Studio is operated from India and primarily intended for users in India, users from other regions may access the service.

By using the service, you acknowledge that your information may be processed and stored in India or other locations where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal data is handled securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
