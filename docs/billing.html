Billing Policy – Drape.Studio

Billing & Refund Policy – Drape.Studio

Last updated: 2026-01-15

1. Pricing Overview

Drape.Studio offers both free and paid plans. Pricing, plan features, and usage limits are displayed on the website or within the application and may vary by plan.

All prices are shown in Indian Rupees (INR) unless otherwise stated.

2. Free Trial (if applicable)

New users may be offered a free trial for selected plans. Trial details, including duration and feature access, are shown at the time of signup.

At the end of the trial period, continued access to paid features requires the purchase of a paid plan.

3. Paid Plans and Billing

Paid plans are purchased as one-time payments and provide access to a defined amount of usage credits or features, as described at the time of purchase.

Drape.Studio does not enable automatic subscription renewals. Users must manually purchase a new plan or credits when their existing usage is exhausted or expires.

By selecting a paid plan and completing payment, you authorize Drape.Studio to charge the applicable fees for your chosen plan.

Billing occurs at the time of purchase.

4. Information Collected at Checkout

When you purchase a paid plan, we may collect certain billing and contact information as required for payment processing, invoicing, and tax compliance.

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Billing address
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address

This information is required by payment gateways operating in India and may be used to:

  • Process payments
  • Calculate applicable taxes (including GST)
  • Generate invoices and receipts
  • Contact you regarding billing-related matters

5. Taxes

Prices may be subject to applicable taxes as required by law.

Customers located in India may be charged Goods and Services Tax (GST).

Customers outside India are generally not charged GST, in accordance with applicable export rules.

Any applicable tax amount will be displayed at checkout or reflected on the invoice.

6. Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely through third-party payment gateways. Drape.Studio does not store credit card details, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Payment gateways may share limited payment-related and contact information with us, such as:

  • Transaction identifiers
  • Payment status
  • Email address and phone number provided at checkout

This information is used only for billing, invoicing, accounting, and customer support purposes.

7. Invoices

Invoices are generated electronically upon successful payment and are made available to users via email or within the application dashboard.

It is the user’s responsibility to ensure that billing information provided is accurate.

8. Failed or Incomplete Payments

If a payment fails or cannot be completed, access to paid features may not be granted until payment is successfully processed.

In the event of a duplicate or failed payment caused by a technical error, users may contact support for review.

9. Refund Policy

Payments made for digital services, usage credits, or plans are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

We do not provide refunds or credits for:

  • Partially used plans or credits
  • Unused or expired credits
  • User inactivity
  • Change of mind after purchase

Refunds may be issued only in cases of:

  • Duplicate charges
  • Proven technical billing errors
  • Other situations required by law

Any approved refund will be processed after verification and at our discretion.

10. No Cancellation or Auto-Renewal

Drape.Studio does not use automatic subscription renewals.

Once a plan or credits are purchased:

  • The purchase remains valid until the credits are used or expire (as described at purchase)
  • There is no recurring billing
  • There is no cancellation requirement

Users may simply choose not to purchase additional plans or credits after their current usage is exhausted.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Billing & Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
